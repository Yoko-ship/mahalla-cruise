# Mahalla Cruise: first phone playtest

This is a pending validation plan. No player study or physical-phone result is
claimed. Use the ARM64 debug APK in `build/android/mahalla-cruise-debug.apk`.
Build/setup instructions are in [ANDROID.md](ANDROID.md).

## Ten-minute session

Invite five people who play casual mobile games in Uzbekistan, including Uzbek
and Russian speakers. Include an older or lower-cost Android phone as well as a
newer one. Record the phone model, Android version, game build, and language;
names and contact details are unnecessary.

1. Open the game and hand over the phone without coaching. Observe whether the
   person starts, steers, collects money, understands a collision, and restarts.
   Record where they hesitate and whether any help was needed.
2. Allow three runs. Ask what caused each crash and whether there was time to
   react. Note missed drags, obscured cars, confusing rewards, and unreadable text.
   Distinguish an intended risk from a collision the player considers unfair.
3. Ask them to pause, change the language and sound, return to play, then switch
   to another app and back. The run should stay paused until they choose Resume.
4. Close and reopen the game. Check the completed best score and preferences.
   Leave the app open for the remainder of the session, noting stutters, heat,
   sound annoyance, and vibration strength. Do not infer battery life from this
   short session.

Ask these neutral questions afterward:

- What felt good, and what was frustrating?
- Which words or controls were unclear?
- Was traffic too quiet, comfortable, or too demanding?
- Would you choose another run now? Why?

## Decisions after the session

Fix crashes, lost saves, blocked controls, clipped text, and unclear collision
causes first. Tune the existing traffic/resource values if several players find
the same part too easy or too demanding. Repeat the affected tasks after a fix.
Use spontaneous replay and specific feedback to choose further work; additional
cars, modes, online features, and monetization remain separate decisions.
